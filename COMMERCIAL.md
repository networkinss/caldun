# Commercial License

Caldun is free for noncommercial use under the [Caldun Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

If your organization uses Caldun for commercial purposes — including internal
business operations, production infrastructure, or any revenue-generating
activity — you need a commercial license.

## What counts as commercial use

- Running Caldun on company hardware or servers
- Including Caldun in a commercial product or service
- Using Caldun as part of paid managed services or consulting work

Charities and educational institutions are covered by the noncommercial license
and do not need a commercial license. Government bodies at any level require a
commercial license.
